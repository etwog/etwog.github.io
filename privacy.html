Privacy Policy for whatif autopost

Last updated: November 2026

whatif autopost is a personal tool used by one creator to upload that creator's own videos to the creator's own YouTube, Instagram and TikTok accounts.

What the tool accesses

When the creator signs in, the tool receives permission from YouTube, Instagram or TikTok to upload videos and read basic account information (such as the account name) for that same account only.

What is stored

Sign-in tokens are stored only on the creator's own computer. Nothing is sent to any server other than YouTube, Instagram and TikTok themselves.

What is not done

The tool does not collect, sell, share or analyse personal data about viewers, followers or any other person. It has no ads, no analytics and no third-party trackers.

Deleting access

The creator can remove the tool's access at any time from the security or connected-apps settings of the YouTube/Google, Instagram or TikTok account. Deleting the tool's sign-in files from the computer also removes the stored tokens.
